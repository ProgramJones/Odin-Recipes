Oatmeal

Description

A healthy bowl of oatmeal loaded with protein and other micronutrients

Ingredients

  • 1 Cup Quick Oats
  • 1/4 Cup Raisins
  • 1 Cup Blueberries
  • 1 Sliced Whole Banana
  • 1 Scoop Protien Powder
  • 1 Tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 Cup Milk

Steps

  1. Mix the oats, raisins, blueberries, protein power, cinnamon, and milk to a bowl.
  2. Microwave the bowl for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the banana.
